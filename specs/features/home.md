# Feature Specification - Home Page

## Overview
The Home page is the landing page for Smart Solar & Security Solutions. It must project a premium, high-tech, and sustainable image immediately to visitors, encouraging them to book a free site visit or initiate contact via WhatsApp.

## Content & Layout

### 1. Hero Section
* **Title**: SMART SOLAR & SECURITY SOLUTIONS
* **Subtitle**: A Full Service Experience
* **Branding Image**: Custom premium solar panels and security camera background (using high-quality design, CSS overlays, and glassmorphism elements).
* **Primary Call-To-Action (CTA) Buttons**:
  1. **Book Free Site Visit**: High-visibility button linking to WhatsApp chat: `https://wa.me/923317383800?text=I%20would%20like%20to%20book%20a%20free%20site%20visit`
  2. **WhatsApp Now**: Button linking to WhatsApp chat: `https://wa.me/923317383800`

### 2. Main Value Proposition Section
* **Heading**: A Full Service Experience
* **Description Paragraphs**:
  1. *"At Smart Solar & Security Solutions, our mission is to empower homes and businesses with reliable solar energy and advanced security systems. We provide tailored solutions for every property, combining high-performance hybrid solar inverters, solar structures, and CCTV security systems to meet your specific energy and safety needs."*
  2. *"We believe that every home and business deserves dependable power and complete protection. That’s why we offer a full-service experience, including system design, professional installation, and ongoing support — ensuring your solar energy and security solutions deliver maximum performance and peace of mind."*

### 3. Core Offerings Grid
A visual, interactive grid showcasing the four major categories:
* **Hybrid Solar Inverters**: Solar inversion and smart power management.
* **Batteries**: Lithium-ion and deep-cycle backup batteries.
* **Solar Panels / Structures**: Premium PV modules and custom mounting structures.
* **CCTV**: HD night-vision and remote-access security cameras.

## Technical Design Details
* **Animations**: Fade-in and slide-up effects on page load, hover scaling on cards, and pulse glow on primary call buttons.
* **Responsive Layout**: Single column on mobile viewports (<640px), transitioning to 2-column or 4-column grids on desktop viewports.
* **Mobile-friendly Header**: Accessible navigation menu toggle (hamburger) displaying page routes.
