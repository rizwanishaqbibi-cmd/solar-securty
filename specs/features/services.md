# Feature Specification - Services Page

## Overview
The Services page lists the comprehensive array of solar energy and smart security system services offered by Smart Solar & Security Solutions. This replaces the default salon templates with highly professional and customized text content.

## Services Catalog

### 1. Hybrid Solar Systems (Sizing & Installation)
* **Description**: Custom solar systems designed to meet your specific load requirements. Includes grid-tied (on-grid), off-grid, and hybrid energy systems with smart energy routing.
* **Key Features**:
  * Professional load calculations and panel array mapping.
  * Quality components from tier-1 brands (e.g., GoodWe, Huawei, Growatt).
  * System integration with existing DB distribution boards.

### 2. Lithium & Tubular Battery Backups
* **Description**: Reliable energy storage options for uninterrupted power supply during load shedding.
* **Key Features**:
  * Next-generation Lithium-Ion (LiFePO4) storage walls with 10+ year lifespan.
  * Specialized deep-cycle tubular battery configurations.
  * Inverter battery-charge profiles optimized for long-term health.

### 3. Customized Solar Mounting Structures
* **Description**: Heavy-duty structural frameworks engineered to withstand high wind speeds and maximize solar irradiance angle.
* **Key Features**:
  * Hot-dip galvanized iron (GI) structures to prevent rust.
  * Raised custom structures for rooftops to save floor space.
  * Correct tilt angle configuration matching Islamabad's coordinates (~33° N).

### 4. CCTV Camera Surveillance Networks
* **Description**: Secure your premises with round-the-clock high-definition surveillance.
* **Key Features**:
  * 4K and 1080p HD IP cameras with color night-vision.
  * Remote mobile app configuration to view live feed from anywhere.
  * Multi-terabyte local Network Video Recorders (NVR) with backup power.

### 5. Net Metering Consultations
* **Description**: Export excess solar energy back to the national grid and offset your electricity bills.
* **Key Features**:
  * Documentation preparation for local DISCO (e.g., IESCO).
  * Net-meter verification, sub-station test oversight, and NEPRA license processing.
  * Bi-directional green meter installation coordination.

### 6. System Auditing & Maintenance
* **Description**: Keep your solar generation and security cameras working at peak efficiency.
* **Key Features**:
  * Solar panel washing, structural bolt checks, and wiring isolation tests.
  * Firmware updates for hybrid inverters.
  * Security camera lens cleaning and NVR storage health check.

## Interface Specifications
* **Layout**: A clean 3-column grid structure utilizing custom cards.
* **Interactions**: Glassmorphism cards that tilt or scale on hover, interactive inquiry modals, or WhatsApp buttons specific to each service type.
* **Mobile Adaptability**: Stacked single-column layouts on mobile screen sizes with tab-based category selection.
