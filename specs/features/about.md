# Feature Specification - About Us Page

## Overview
The About page tells the history, vision, and leadership of Smart Solar & Security Solutions, positioning them as a trusted, veteran contractor in Islamabad since 2010.

## Page Content

### 1. Company History & Mission
* **Founded**: 2010 by Irfan Ahmad.
* **Service Area**: Islamabad/Rawalpindi metro area.
* **Core Philosophy**: Delivering reliable, efficient, and safe energy systems that empower residential and commercial clients while accelerating sustainable energy adoption in Pakistan.

### 2. Key Achievements & Milestones
* **100+ Completed Projects**: High-quality hybrid solar systems, custom mounting, and commercial camera networks.
* **Professional Audits**: Expert system diagnostics, custom tilt brackets, and net metering licensing.
* **Trusted Quality**: Strong focus on customer satisfaction and quality installation standard.

### 3. Supported Technologies & Brands
We showcase compatibility with leading globally recognized industry brands:
* **Hybrid Inverters**: GoodWe, Huawei, Growatt, Solis.
* **Solar Panels**: Jinko, Longi, Canadian Solar (Tier-1 modules).
* **Batteries**: Narada, Pylontech, custom tubular batteries.
* **CCTV Systems**: Hikvision, Dahua, Uniview.

### 4. Leadership / Corporate Info
* **Founder & Lead Engineer**: Irfan Ahmad
* **Company Structure**: Dedicated team of design engineers, electrical technicians, and metal fabrication specialists.

## Design & Style Specs
* **Aesthetics**: Sleek timeline design representing the 16-year journey (2010 to 2026), and a dedicated grid displaying logos of trusted manufacturers.
* **Responsive Layout**: Two-column flex container (image/text split) converting to single column on devices with width less than 768px.
